# Changelog

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-24)

- Notices above a message (blocked images, read receipts, invitations, parcel and booking cards) share
  one compact layout: a 28 px icon, label and title on one line, actions at the end of that line.
- Attachments are one 32 px line each; in a wide reading pane the count, the files and Download all
  share a single line.
- The heading of a plugin's sidebar section matches Folders and Tags.
- "Always trust this sender" reads "Trust sender" (English only).
- Floating hover actions are smaller, and team mailbox and plugin actions in them are drawn like the
  built-in ones.

## 1.4.1 (2026-11-24)

First release in the Bulwark Extensions directory.

- Warm Paper (light) and Graphite (dark) grounds, Geist and Geist Mono type, ink actions.
- An always-dark navigation rail in both modes.
- The Sunrise gradient kept for small marks: what is current, what is unread, and Compose.
